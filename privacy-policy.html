Privacy Policy

AddictionInterrupter is designed to add a brief pause before you open certain apps. This policy explains how the app handles your information: it doesn't collect any. Data we collect: None. AddictionInterrupter does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Data leaving your device: None. The app runs entirely on your device through the Shortcuts app. It does not send any information to us or to any third party, and we operate no servers that receive your data. Third parties: AddictionInterrupter does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software.
